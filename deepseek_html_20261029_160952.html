Would You Use an AI Therapist?

Corporate English Lesson

1. Think about these questions before watching

  • Many people use technology to stay healthy or manage stress. What kind of apps or digital tools do you currently use for your well-being, and what do you find most helpful or challenging about them?
  • Artificial intelligence is becoming more common in many aspects of our lives. In your opinion, what are some areas where AI could be genuinely beneficial, and where should we be more cautious, especially when it comes to sensitive topics like mental health?
  • Imagine a future where AI-powered tools are widely used to support mental health. What do you think would be the biggest advantages of this? What potential drawbacks or ethical concerns might arise if people relied on AI for therapy?

2. Vocabulary (Click to flip)

reframe negative thoughts

To change the way you think about a situation, especially by looking at it from a more positive or constructive perspective.

"AI can help individuals reframe negative thoughts and provide advice."
a double-edged sword

Something that has both advantages and disadvantages, or that can have both good and bad consequences.

"AI in therapy can be a double-edged sword, offering convenience but lacking human empathy."
foster a connection

To help develop a relationship or a feeling of understanding and trust between people.

"A human therapist can foster a deeper connection than a chatbot."
raise ethical questions

To bring up moral issues or dilemmas that need to be considered and discussed.

"This situation raises ethical questions about inclusivity and safety."
narrow cultural view

A limited understanding or perspective that doesn't consider the beliefs, values, or experiences of people from different cultures.

"If AI models are trained on Western data, they might possess a narrow cultural view."

5. Deep Discussion

  • Is AI therapy a double-edged sword for mental health support? Why?
  • Should AI therapists be regulated? Who is responsible if the AI gives bad advice?
  • If AI therapy is cheaper, is it ethical to prioritise it over human therapy for some groups?
  • What happens to data privacy when you share your secrets with an AI chatbot?
  • Do you think AI models with a narrow cultural view could cause harm to minority groups?
  • Can AI genuinely foster a connection essential for therapy?
  • Do you think people can feel real empathy from a machine? Why might someone prefer it?
  • Is a superficial interaction sometimes better than no interaction at all?
  • If an AI helps someone reframe negative thoughts, does it matter that the AI doesn't "feel"?
  • Would you trust an AI to know when a person is in a crisis?
  • As IT professionals, do you think the tech industry pushes AI too fast without thinking about ethical questions?
  • How would you feel if your company offered an AI therapist for employee wellbeing?
  • Would you trust an AI built by a tech company with your mental health?
  • If you were building an AI therapist, what would be the biggest challenge to make it truly foster a connection?
  • Do tech companies have a responsibility to ensure their AI doesn't have a narrow cultural view?
